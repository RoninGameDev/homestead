Home Screen & One-Time Setup

🔑 Do we have to log in every time?

Never! There is no annoying Google login popup or password prompt when you open the app. You set your Household Key and choose Amy or Tom once on each phone. Your phone stores that key forever in local device memory so Homestead opens straight to your lists in 0.1 seconds.

🍎 Amy's iPhone (Safari)
  1. Open this link in Safari on her iPhone.
  2. Tap Share (square with arrow at bottom) → "Add to Home Screen".
  3. It defaults to Amy (Blue) right out of the box!
🤖 Tom's Android Phone (Chrome)
  1. Open this link in Chrome on your Android.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu → "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap Tom (Red) at the top right once, and your phone remembers you forever.
